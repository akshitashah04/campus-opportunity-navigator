# Discovery research plan

**Goal:** Learn how graduate students actually find, assess, and track university opportunities. Do not pitch the prototype until after the main interview.

## Recruit

Speak with five graduate students who searched for a university job, research role, teaching role, or fellowship in the last three months. Include people with different outcomes and channels. Ask permission before recording; otherwise take notes without names.

## Interview guide (20–25 minutes)

1. Think of the last opportunity you considered. How did you first hear about it?
2. Walk me through what you did from discovery to deciding whether to apply.
3. Where else did you look during that search? What prompted you to switch channels?
4. What information did you need before deciding? Was anything hard to verify?
5. What happened with the deadline? How did you keep track of it?
6. Show or describe how you track opportunities and applications now.
7. Tell me about a role you did not apply to. What stopped you?
8. What part of the process took the most time or effort?
9. What already works well for you?

Follow up with “What happened next?”, “Can you show me?”, and “How often has that happened?” Avoid “Would you use my app?”

## Synthesis

For each interview, distinguish **what the person did** from **what they said they might do**. Group repeated observations, count how many participants encountered each issue, note the severity, and keep counterexamples. Update `product-brief.md` with evidence and a decision: proceed, narrow, or change direction.
